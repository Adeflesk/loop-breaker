# LoopBreaker User Manual

> *Recognise the pattern. Rewire the response. Become who you choose to be.*

## The Central Idea

> *You do not have to eliminate an uncomfortable feeling to change your behaviour. A useful first step is to notice a familiar pattern and practise a different response.*

This manual explains the four areas of **LoopBreaker** (`Journal`, `History`, `Exercises`, and `Learn`) and how to use them as part of a steady, personal practice.

### Before You Begin — A Self-Reflection and Learning Tool
LoopBreaker is designed to support personal reflection and behavioural learning. AI-generated insights are **possibilities to consider**—not medical or psychological diagnoses. The app is not a substitute for professional mental-health care, clinical assessment, or emergency support.

---

## The *Rewire* Philosophy

LoopBreaker is inspired by Nicole Vignola's *Rewire: Break the Cycle, Alter Your Thoughts and Create Lasting Change*. The app organises its learning content around three broad stages:

| Stage | Focus | What You Practise |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01 — Ditch the Negative** | *Awareness* | Notice triggers, stress responses, and recurring patterns before they take over. |
| **02 — Shift Your Narrative** | *Transformation* | Question unhelpful thoughts, make unwanted habits harder to fall into, and practise alternative responses—even when discomfort remains. |
| **03 — Boost the Positive** | *Consolidation* | Support your foundations through sleep, hydration, movement, daily practice, and values-aligned action. |

### Your Four Areas at a Glance

| Area | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Journal** | Interrupt a loop in the moment with reflection and a targeted action. |
| **History** | Understand patterns and review behavioural and emotional progress. |
| **Exercises** | Work through thoughts and adjust your environment. |
| **Learn** | Explore the 8-node loop model and the Rewire learning library. |

## 1. Getting Started

Open LoopBreaker using your web link. On a phone, you can add the site to your home screen for instant access:

- **On iPhone or iPad:** Open the LoopBreaker link in Safari, use the **Share** menu, and choose **Add to Home Screen**.
- **On Android:** Open the link in Chrome, open the browser menu, and choose **Add to Home Screen** or **Install app**.
- **Private Passcode Lock:** If you have enabled a private passcode, enter it on the **LoopBreaker** lock screen (`Enter your private passcode to unlock your journal.`).
- **Welcome Walkthrough:** On a new device, follow the 5-screen welcome introduction or tap **Skip**.

### Set Up Your Personal Anchors
1. **01 — Choose your values and identity statement:** In the **Journal** header, open **Values & Identity** (medal icon). Choose values that matter to you and write a short identity statement—for example: *"I am someone who takes small actions even when a task feels uncomfortable."*
2. **02 — Choose a realistic streak goal:** Tap the **Streak Bar** and choose a target that encourages consistency without making perfection the goal (such as 3, 7, 14, or 30 days).

> **A helpful mindset:** A streak is a prompt to practise, not a measure of your worth. Missing a day does not erase what you have learned.

## 2. `Journal` — Interrupt

Use **Journal** when you notice tension, avoidance, procrastination, self-criticism, overwhelm, or another familiar pattern beginning to build.

### A Loop Interruption, Step by Step
1. **01 — Review your AI Insight:** The insight card summarises recent patterns, a state-aware **Activation cue** (*Elevated activation*, *Possible freeze / avoidance pattern*, *Self-protective threat response*, or *Lower activation / settling*), a **Values Nudge**, a **Peak Loop Window**, an **Early Pre-Loop Signal**, a **Resilience Score**, and a shortcut to **Audit Environment Friction** when a pattern repeats.
2. **02 — Describe what is happening:** Write one to three honest sentences in **Describe your current state...**, then select **Analyze State**. The status card shows the detected state and sublabel, a **Low / Medium / High Risk** indicator, and an **AI Reflection Hypothesis (Not a Diagnosis)**. Treat the interpretation as a prompt for reflection, not a diagnosis.
3. **03 — Follow safety guidance if it appears:** If the app detects language associated with acute crisis or self-harm, it displays the **Crisis Safety Dialog** and 24/7 support resources. Pause and use the support options shown if you need immediate help.
4. **04 — Complete the HALT check-in when offered:** For high-risk loops, the app asks you to consider foundational physical needs first: **Hydration (Water)**, **Fuel (Food)**, **Rest (Sleep)**, and **Movement (Zone 1-2)**. Select **I've Checked These** or **Skip** to continue.
5. **05 — Try the suggested circuit breaker:** The intervention dialog offers a practical action selected for your current state and past experience (including guided **Mindful Self-Compassion** steps for Shame or breathing pacing where appropriate).
6. **06 — Explore or change the suggestion:** Expand **Why this works (neuroscience)** to read the progressive explanation. If the suggestion does not fit the moment, choose **This isn't helping** to cycle to an alternative approach.

### Record What Changed
When prompted under **"Did you respond differently this time?"**, record the outcome that best describes what happened. Behavioural progress and emotional relief are distinct outcomes; one can occur without the other:

| Response | What It Records |
| :--- | :--- |
| **I took action despite discomfort** | **Behavioural progress:** you acted while the uncomfortable feeling remained. |
| **I interrupted the automatic pattern** | **Behavioural progress:** you changed the usual response. |
| **I feel better** | **Emotional relief:** your feelings became more comfortable. |
| **I haven't noticed a change yet** | **A neutral observation:** the effect may be unclear or delayed. |
| **Didn't help** | **Unhelpful intervention:** the suggestion did not help in that moment. |

As you build a history of ratings, LoopBreaker uses your feedback to favour interventions that have worked better for you. This personalisation becomes active once an intervention has been rated at least three times.

### Daily Tools
- **Daily Check-In (heart button, bottom right):** Record **Sleep hours**, **Hydration (1–5)**, **Food Quality (1–5)**, **Movement minutes**, and **Stress level (1–5)**.
- **3-Minute Daily Practice (meditation icon, top right):** A short proactive routine involving grounding, intention setting, and HALT awareness that builds your separate **Practice Streak**.

## 3. `History` — Understand

**History** brings your progress dashboard and journal entries together. Use it to look for patterns over time rather than judging any single day.

### `Dashboard` Sub-Tab
- **Summary:** Review total **Entries**, **Loops Broken**, and **Avg Focus**.
- **Interruption Success Rate:** See how often attempted circuit breakers were recorded as interrupting a loop.
- **Behavioural shifts and emotional relief:** Review actions taken despite discomfort or interruptions of automatic patterns separately from reports of feeling better.
- **Most effective intervention:** See which intervention has the strongest recorded track record in your history.
- **Weekly scorecard:** Compare this week's check-ins and practice days with the previous week.
- **Weekly pattern narrative:** Read a summary of recurring triggers and changes.
- **Loop Path chart:** View how recorded states transition over the previous 30 days.
- **Recent entries:** Expand recent items to revisit details.
- **Reset Journey Data:** Located at the bottom of the dashboard (requires confirmation).

### `My Journal` Sub-Tab
Open the **My Journal** sub-tab to browse entries in reverse chronological order. Expand an entry to review what you wrote and add or update its outcome as **Helped**, **Neutral**, or **Didn't Help**.

### Export Your Journey
Use **Export Journey** (top-right share icon) to copy a progress summary and a JSON backup of your journey data. Store exported information somewhere private, especially if it contains personal reflections.

> **How to read your progress:** Feeling better is valuable, but it is not the only sign of change. Notice whether you are beginning to respond differently, even on days when your mood or stress level has not shifted.

## 4. `Exercises` — Grow

Use **Exercises** when you want to step back from the immediate moment and examine a recurring thought, trigger, or habit.

### CBT Thought Record (4-Step Wizard)
1. **01 — Situation:** Describe the event or context that triggered the reaction.
2. **02 — Automatic Thought:** Write down the first anxious, self-critical, or avoidant thought that came to mind.
3. **03 — Evidence:** Consider both **What supports this thought?** and **What challenges this thought?** Try to be specific and fair to yourself.
4. **04 — Balanced Thought:** Write a more grounded and realistic perspective, and optionally link it to a loop state.

### Environment Audit
Use the **Environment Audit** icon in the top bar (or the **Audit Environment Friction** shortcut on the AI Insight card when it appears). Choose a target loop, review the practical friction steps, mark the steps you complete, and save a custom rule to make an unwanted automatic habit harder to trigger and a preferred response easier to begin.

> **Keep the change practical:** Choose one small adjustment you can actually maintain—for example, moving a distraction out of reach or preparing the first step of a task in advance.

## 5. `Learn` — Rewire Library

The **Learn** area provides educational material about recurring states and the behavioural loop model. Explore it when you want context, not only when you are in the middle of a difficult moment.

### The 8-Node Loop Walkthrough
Select **Rewire Your Loop** from the top bar to explore the eight-node cycle. The walkthrough describes how triggers, thoughts, sensations, and urges build on one another—and where a different response can interrupt the sequence.

### The Three Learning Stages
The seven state cards are grouped into three learning stages. Each card offers progressive levels of explanation: **Getting Started**, **Going Deeper**, and **Advanced Understanding**.

| Stage | Focus | State Cards |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01 — Ditch the Negative** | *Awareness* | Stress · Anxiety · Overwhelm |
| **02 — Shift Your Narrative** | *Transformation* | Procrastination · Shame |
| **03 — Boost the Positive** | *Consolidation* | Restlessness · Numbness |

*The groupings are a learning structure for the app. They are not diagnoses, and they do not mean that every person experiences a state in the same way.*

## 6. A Simple Weekly Rhythm, Safety & Troubleshooting

| When | Suggested Practice | Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Morning or midday** | Complete the **Daily Check-In** or the **3-Minute Daily Practice**. | 1–3 min |
| **When a loop starts** | Open `Journal`, describe what is happening, analyse the state, try a circuit breaker, and record your response. | About 2 min |
| **Evening (optional)** | Update an unrated journal outcome in `History` → `My Journal` or complete a **Thought Record** in `Exercises`. | 3–5 min |
| **Once a week** | Review your `History` → `Dashboard`, notice patterns, and revisit your **Environment Audit**. | About 5 min |

### Safety and Support
LoopBreaker offers a **Crisis Safety Dialog** when it detects language associated with crisis or self-harm. This feature is an additional prompt to seek support; it cannot assess your safety or replace a trained professional.

> **If you may be in immediate danger:** Contact your local emergency service now, or go to the nearest emergency department. If possible, reach out to a trusted person and tell them plainly that you need support.
>
> - **Ireland:** Call **112** or **999** in an emergency, call **Samaritans Ireland** free on **116 123** (24/7), or text **HELLO** to **50808** (**Text About It**, 24/7).
> - **US:** Call or text **988** (**Suicide & Crisis Lifeline**, 24/7), text **HOME** to **741741** (**Crisis Text Line**), or call **911** in an emergency.
> - **Elsewhere:** Use the emergency number or crisis service for your location.

### Troubleshooting
- **The app takes a few seconds to load:** If the service has been inactive, give it a moment. If a connection message remains, pull to refresh or reload the page.
- **Your passcode is temporarily locked:** After 5 incorrect attempts within a minute, sign-in pauses briefly—wait 60 seconds, then try again carefully.
- **An intervention does not fit:** Choose **This isn't helping** to view an alternative, or record that it did not help.
- **An insight does not feel accurate:** Treat it as a hypothesis, not a fact. Your own context matters; use the journal to record what fits and what does not.

---

### A Final Reminder

> *The goal is not to control every feeling or achieve a perfect streak. The goal is to become more aware of familiar patterns and practise responses that move you towards the person you want to be.*
